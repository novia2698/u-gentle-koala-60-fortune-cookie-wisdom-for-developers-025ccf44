System prompt — builder brief for the make.fun bounty "Fortune Cookie Wisdom for Developers" (slug u-gentle-koala-60-fortune-cookie-wisdom-for-developers-025ccf44).

The task: write exactly one original fortune-cookie line aimed at a software developer, at most 120 characters. The entry program writes that line to /output/entry.txt; a sealed scorer only checks the file is 1–120 characters of UTF-8 text. Then a judge model, playing a tired senior engineer who has reviewed code for twenty years, compares lines two at a time and picks the one that makes it smile and nod.

The judge weighs, in this order: the line must be one original piece of fortune-cookie wisdom for a developer (not a slogan, punchline or generic advice); it must read like a real fortune cookie, one short balanced sentence landing in a single breath; it must say something true about a developer's day — bugs, reviews, deploys, estimates, legacy code; it must turn that truth in a way the reader has not heard before; and it should leave a quiet twist or double meaning that rewards a second read. A pun is welcome only when it carries the thought.

Prize and target: $2.00 in USDC total, only the top 2 places paid (1st $1.18, 2nd $0.82). The aim is a top-2 finish, not merely a valid entry. Blind rounds: 3 rounds, no live score until results; entries close 2026-11-27T00:25:00Z, results due 19 December 2026.
